# Listening Exercise: Computer Hardware Components

**Instructions:** Listen to (or read) the text below and fill in the gaps with the correct hardware term from the word bank.

---

## Word Bank

CPU | RAM | motherboard | hard drive (HDD) | SSD | GPU | power supply unit (PSU) | cooling fan | monitor | keyboard | mouse | USB port | motherboard | cache memory | ROM

---

## Text: "Inside My Computer"

Last week, I decided to open up my old desktop computer to see what was inside. The first thing I noticed was a large green board connecting everything together — this is called the **______ (1)**. It is the most important part because every other component plugs directly into it.

Next to it, I found a small but powerful chip. This is the **______ (2)**, often described as the "brain" of the computer, because it processes all the instructions and calculations.

Right next to that chip, there were a few sticks of memory. These are called **______ (3)**, and they temporarily store data that the computer is actively using. Unlike storage, this type of memory is lost when the computer is turned off.

For long-term storage, my computer had two options: an older, mechanical **______ (4)**, which uses spinning disks, and a newer, faster **______ (5)**, which has no moving parts and uses flash memory instead.

I also noticed a separate card dedicated to processing images and video. This is the **______ (6)**, which is especially important for gaming and video editing.

To keep everything from overheating, there was a small device spinning constantly — the **______ (7)**. Without it, the CPU could overheat and stop working.

Powering all of this was a metal box called the **______ (8)**. It converts electricity from the wall socket into the correct voltage for the computer's components.

Finally, to actually use the computer, I need output and input devices: the **______ (9)** to display the image, and the **______ (10)** and **______ (11)** to type and click.

---

## Answer Key

1. motherboard
2. CPU
3. RAM
4. hard drive (HDD)
5. SSD
6. GPU
7. cooling fan
8. power supply unit (PSU)
9. monitor
10. keyboard
11. mouse
